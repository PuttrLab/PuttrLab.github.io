2. Description of Service

PuttrLab provides a mobile application that uses your iPhone's camera to track a visual marker attached to a putter head and calculate putting stroke metrics. The App is available as a free download with an optional one-time in-app purchase to unlock the Pro tier.

  • Free tier: Includes putt analysis with face angle and tempo metrics, stroke replay, and storage of your three most recent sessions.
  • PuttrLab Pro (one-time in-app purchase): Unlocks all five stroke metrics (face angle, tempo, acceleration, path symmetry, and contact point), unlimited session history with trend tracking, personalized coaching tips and drill recommendations after every session, full session configuration, and session tagging.

PuttrLab also provides a free, printable tracking marker for use with the App, available for download on the Website.
